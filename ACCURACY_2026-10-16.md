## 2. Pipeline and cohort (protocol fixed before running)

- **Cohort (identical for every model).**
  - Board lines (platform set, standard line type); the latest forecast logged at least 30 minutes before the start;
    settled; pushes and voids out.
  - One row per unit (player x match x stat x scope): the P1 rule.
  - Test set: 1,195 units / 72 matches on test days 2026-10-12..16. 10-11 is training only.
- **Kept separate:** member-entered lines (1,257 rows / 75 matches, live log loss 0.581 - easier, off-market lines) and
  adjusted goblin / demon lines (69 rows / 12 matches).
- **Chronological.** Rolling origin by match day: each test day is predicted from fits on earlier days only, so every
  row of a match stays in one split. The ridge strength is chosen inside the training days (leave-last-day-out).
  Intervals are 95%, with whole matches resampled.
- **Compared:**
  - LIVE: the published chance.
  - BASE: the earlier days' Over rate per stat, shrunk to 50%. FLAT 50% is also shown.
  - The existing challengers: lb-001 everywhere; pl-2 where its logged anchors exist (497 units).
  - CORR: the one candidate, `logit P(Over) = a_s + b_s * z`.
    - z = (projection - line) / (c_s * sqrt(projection)): the independent projection's standardized disagreement with
      the line.
    - Fitted per stat (kills, HS), ridge-shrunk toward 50%.
  - Development-only variants of CORR: + thin history, + last-10 form, + platform, and on logit(live chance) instead of z.
- **Existing work checked first.** fh-1 (full HLTV history) is already the full-history learner, but its pre-match records
  only began today, so it has no development rows on this cohort. lb-001 and pl-2 are the strongest existing
  challengers here. None of them has passed a review.

## 4. What this means for confidence (owner point 4)

The evidence says the live chances overstate confidence at real lines. A validated correction would be a legitimate
improvement even if it turned B grades into Leans. But the only correction tested, CORR, does not beat the simple
line-based baseline. Its whole benefit is moving toward 50%, so it has not established a validated correction. The
promotion rule is the registered forward review (300 / 600 / 1,000 distinct forward matches); it is not bypassed. Live
grades, chances and stakes are unchanged. Stakes were not raised and no Over/Under balance was forced.

## 6. Not proven

- **No candidate makes better picks at real offered lines.** Live's B and better recommendations hit about 50% on 68
  matches. No tested correction found a subset that beats 50% with an interval that excludes it.
- **fh-1's forward value is unknown.** It may improve the projection: it was better on 12 months of reconstructed
  history, but only +0.0015 on archived book lines. On kills, the line currently beats the projection.
- **Only six days of real-line data.** 72 test matches is small, and 5 of the 6 days were inspected repeatedly. A real
  effect of a few points of hit rate would not be visible yet.
- **The Over / Under split** (Overs 42.9%, Unders 56.4% in the live subset) is a development observation, not a rule.
  Acting on it would force a side balance. The forward review will show whether it persists.
