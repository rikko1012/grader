# Accuracy investigation - 2026-10-15

Scripts: `research/accuracy_2026_10_15/` (read-only database dump, as-of dataset, comparisons). Everything below is
**development evidence**: every period through 2026-10-15 has been inspected. Nothing here changed a live grade.

**What this study is and is not (corrected 2026-10-15, owner review):**
- It **reconstructs** forecasts from database rows dated before each match. That is not the same as forecasts saved
  before the match, and none of it is a forward forecast.
- Its "current" arm only **approximates** the live history pin: it reproduces 74% of pinned production forecasts within
  0.5% (section 1).
- It is **supporting evidence**. The product test is fh-1's forecasts saved before each match, judged on archived book
  lines after settlement (section 6). It is not a substitute for an improvement on the props members can play, and it
  does not show that full history fixes prop accuracy.

## 1. Data and reproduction

- **Source:** the HLTV database (all tiers, the 2026-10-13 backfill included), dumped read-only month by month.
- **Population:** every player x match with Maps 1-2 played by 10 players on both maps, Nov 2025 - Oct 15 2026.
  - **97,651 player-series / 9,778 matches.** 43,588 are in the original top-200 set and 54,063 are lower-tier
    matches that only the backfill brought in.
  - Excluded: maps without 10 players (substitutions); HS targets with a 0-headshot / 12+-kill row (feed error); Map 1
    and Maps 1-2 only (Maps 1-3 not scored here).
  - Scored months: Jan - Oct 2026 (85,391 rows / 8,550 matches); Nov - Dec 2025 is burn-in for anything fitted.
- **Inputs, strictly pre-match:**
  - The live perf-2 formula (perf.PARAMS), using only maps from earlier days inside the live 420-day window.
  - Player kills / rounds (all, tonight's environment), headshots per kill, the opponent's kills conceded, and as-of
    Elo for slices.
  - No ranking or roster from the future enters.
- **Two history views:**
  - **current**: the old top-200 history the live pin reads (N0, approximated as the original set + arrivals from
    2026-10-09).
  - **full**: every match in the database.
- **Reproduction against production forecasts:**
  - The full view reproduces 11,705 forecasts made while the repaired history was live: 99% within 0.5%.
  - The current view reproduces 425 pinned forecasts: median gap 0.17%, 74% within 0.5%. The live inclusion rule is
    only approximated, so the "current" arm below is close to the live inputs but is not the live selection. The fh-1
    shadow (section 6) uses the live forecast's exact rows instead.
- **Archived book lines:** only real archived lines (board standard lines with feed ids, latest forecast 30+ min before
  start), Oct 11-15: 656 units / 41 matches. No historical line is invented.

## 3. The strongest supported cause

**The performance model reads a starved history.** The pinned top-200 history leaves many players with little or no
history. The shrinkage then pulls them toward 0.93 x the pro average, and their opponents' conceded kills toward average.
For players who meet weaker opposition, both pulls go downward. The result is a systematic under-projection that
concentrates where history is thin, opponents are weak and matches are online - and it is spread across every month.

- **Expected rounds:** the two simple as-of rounds models tested (league mean; both teams' recent map length) are worse

## 4. Why confidence does not rank outcomes on book lines

**The path.**
- Projection -> discretised normal (sd = 1.457 sqrt(lam) kills, beta-binomial HS) -> raw Over / Under / push -> P(Over |
  no push) -> the kills cap at 22-78% -> the displayed chance (3 decimals) -> the grade (existing thresholds) -> stake
  rules.
- perf-2 lines have no Over margin (old pipeline only). **On the 336 archived kills lines the cap changed no
  forecast:** raw and published log-loss are 0.7077 vs 0.7078. The displayed percentage carries no decision penalty
  here.

**Calibration of the predictive distribution (history, no lines).**
- The central 50% / 80% intervals hold 48.6-51.9% / 77.4-81.1% per month (current) and 50.0-52.8% / 79.0-81.7% (full).
- This is broadly compatible with nominal coverage in this sample. It does not prove the spread is right for every
  player or line.
- Calibration slope of actual on projection: current 0.92, full 1.06.

**Discrimination of totals (history).** The rank correlation of projection with actual kills is **0.203 current ->
0.294 full**. The full history ranks players' totals clearly better.

**At the book's lines (archived, 41 matches).** No version separates winners from losers:

| model | units | log-loss | AUC | gain vs live log-loss (95%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| live as published | 656 | 0.7015 | 0.516 | - |
| current recomputed | 656 | 0.6994 | 0.527 | +0.0021 (-0.0054 to +0.0104) |
| full history | 656 | 0.7000 | 0.526 | +0.0015 (-0.0054 to +0.0101) |
| lb-001 | 656 | | 0.514 | +0.0098 (-0.0048 to +0.0241) |
| match_m1 | 656 | | 0.520 | +0.0037 (-0.0074 to +0.0150) |
| rr-1 | 353 / 22 matches | | 0.503 | +0.0024 (-0.0084 to +0.0126) |

- Projection MAE on these lines: current 4.90, full 4.88, **the book's line 4.82**.
- The board's lines sit mostly where history is deep, which is where the full history gains least (the top-200 set:
  +0.004). There, the projection's departures from the line have not shown reliable predictive value in this sample.
- **A better projection on history has not yet produced better probabilities at book lines (41 matches).** That needs
  forecasts saved before forward matches.
- **Calibration is a separate route to accuracy (corrected 2026-10-15).** Better calibration can improve probability
  accuracy (log-loss, Brier) even when the point projections stay unchanged: a printed 60% that happens 55% of the time
  is a worse probability than a printed 55%. lb-001 tests one recalibration on the same lines. It is not part of fh-1,
  which changes only the history.

## 5. The candidate

**fh-1 - full HLTV history for the performance model.** The live perf-2 formula and parameters, unchanged (perf.PARAMS,
SPREAD, cap, grades, stakes), with the performance model reading **every match in the HLTV database (all tiers)** instead
of the pinned top-200 history (N0, rule n0-r4).
- One change, no new parameter.
- It is exactly the "repaired history" that the owner pinned out after the unintended activation; the opponent-strength
  arm planned with it is not needed for the gain.
- Candidates tried and NOT taken: every other row in the table in section 3.
- The Elo team-strength term on top of full history is mixed: it helps underdogs (big underdog bias +1.79 -> +1.25; MAE
  +0.109) and hurts favourites (big favourite +0.26 -> +0.86; MAE -0.078). Not combined; it is a possible later test,
  with a non-linear form defined before its data.

**Uncertainty that remains:**
- The overall gain is small: kills MAE 6.34 -> 6.21 (about 2%), mainly from thin-history players.
- On the original top-200 set (established players) the kills MAE gain is not demonstrated (+0.004, interval across 0).
  The gain is driven by thin-history and lower-tier players.
- The full history over-projects underdogs (+0.82 / +1.79) and players facing strong opponents (+0.57). The opponent
  adjustment does not fully cancel the weak-opposition maps it adds.
- On archived book lines (41 matches) the line probabilities are not better (+0.0015, interval across 0).
- The 2026-10-13 staged replay (23 matches of board lines) found no gain and a larger Over bias. It is consistent with
  this: those lines are mostly deep-history players.
- All of this is development data.

## 6. Deployment status and forward plan (revised 2026-10-15, owner review)

- **fh-1 runs as a production shadow only** (`perf_shadow.py`; CHALLENGERS.md fh-1). Live grades, the N0 pin, the live
  grader and every existing challenger definition are unchanged.
- **Same production code, one difference.** Live and fh-1 go through the production projection path (perf.project,
  perf_live.projection's post-processing, the BO3.gg fallback rule, grader.grade_leg, the verdict). They differ only in
  history eligibility.
  - Live arm: the published forecast's **exact** rows (the live cache, N0 rule n0-r4), not this study's approximation.
  - fh-1: those rows plus the rows N0 excludes, from the same query.
- **Saved before each match.** With each board forecast the shadow saves:
  - both arms' projection, chance, side, grade and stake;
  - the input snapshot and grader inputs (content-addressed);
  - the history revisions, the read times and the logging time.
- **Checks on every record:**
  - the live arm rebuilt from the snapshot reproduces the published forecast exactly;
  - when full history adds no row, the two arms are identical;
  - each record replays exactly from its saved inputs.
- **Evaluation after settlement, on P1 archived book lines.** Per unit, the latest record logged 30+ min before the start
  is scored. Reported:
  - paired log-loss and Brier;
  - projection RMSE (the proper score for a mean), MAE (comparable with this study) and bias;
  - unique-match counts;
  - "thin history / lower tier" and "established" separately (registered before any fh-1 result: established = 600+
    time-weighted N0 rounds and an opponent with N0 history).
- **Retrospective reconstruction** from earlier-dated database rows (this study's method) may supplement the analysis. It
  is never labelled an exact forward forecast.
- **Promotion:** the registered rules are unchanged (300 / 600 / 1,000 distinct forward matches; owner decision). Any
  amendment needs the owner's explicit, prospective approval.
- **No accuracy gain is claimed at book lines** until settled results on saved forecasts show one.
