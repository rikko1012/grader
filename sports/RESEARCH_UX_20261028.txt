Sports research workflow comparison and shipped improvements

Sources reviewed (public product pages/documentation, not paid-account audits):
Outlier saved filters: https://help.outlier.bet/en/articles/9425417-using-filters-to-define-your-sports-betting-strategy
Outlier prop research: https://help.outlier.bet/en/articles/6711738-research-player-propositions-with-outlier-s-prop-finder
Props.cash: https://props.cash/
Pine Sports: https://www.pine-sports.com/

Scout already has a dense prop board, player history, exact-line book comparisons,
research forecasts, comparison selections, slip building and tracked picks. Those
are the same broad research workflows emphasized by competing tools. The useful
gap addressed here is repeatability: users previously had to recreate combinations
of stat, book, game, side and player filters manually.

Implemented:
- Named saved filter views, separate NBA and NFL lists, up to 12 per league.
- Save, apply and remove from a compact disclosure above the board.
- Duplicate-name protection, safe handling of broken browser storage, bounded
  names and filter values. All user-visible names are escaped.
- Expired games and unavailable books reset to All with an explanation.
- Saved views preserve filters, not frozen prices or projections. Data continues
  to come from the normal live snapshot. Browser-local storage is stated clearly.
- Responsive controls, 44px action targets, keyboard focus and status messages.
- Missing essential page scripts now show a Reload Sports recovery screen instead
  of throwing during initialization and leaving the workspace blank.

Validation:
Sports suite: 797 passed, 68 skipped (including browser-dependent tests), 101 warnings.
New Node coverage: league isolation, malformed data, duplicate names, limits,
filter normalization and missing-script recovery. Existing UX helper tests passed.
Actual Chrome fixture checks: save, reset, apply, reload persistence, remove;
desktop and 390px phone layout inspected. Fixture data never enters production.

Further opportunities, not claimed shipped:
- Saved player watchlists and opt-in line-change alerts, with server-side revision
  and freshness checks before notification.
- Research notes attached to immutable tracked picks.
- Account-synced views across devices (requires authenticated storage and limits).
- Expanded injury/rotation context only when sources and update cadence support it.
Do not copy competitor EV/accuracy claims or convert research confidence into
validated grades. These UI changes do not alter model probabilities, settlement,
validation gates, stakes, member access or paid hosting resources.
