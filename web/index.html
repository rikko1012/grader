How grades work

Built from official pro matches. Maps 1–2 lines (most of the board) use our performance model on every official map a player has played on HLTV: his kills per round, with recent months counting most (a map from four months ago counts half), weighted toward his numbers in tonight's setting (LAN or online), against how many kills the opponent gives up. Headshot lines add his headshots per kill. A player with a short history is pulled toward the pro average until his own maps take over. FACEIT games never count.

Map 1 and Maps 1–3 lines still use the older model (his BO3.gg rates and form, the maps when known, win chance, opponent rank, head-to-head, Round Swing, and a small cushion an Over must clear): Map 1 is half of its Maps 1–2 projection, and Maps 1–3 adds a third map only as often as the series is expected to go 1–1.

Projection → chance → grade. The projection is compared with the line (a 15.5 line needs 16 for an Over, 15 or less for an Under; on a whole line like 16, exactly 16 is a push) and turned into a chance from how much a player's totals normally swing from match to match. Kills chances are capped at 78% because big kill totals swing more than any model expects.

Stake rules change the units, never the chance. If his last 10 matches clearly disagree at this line, the stake drops to 0 (FORM FIGHT) or 0.5U (TAPE FIGHT). Stand-ins (max 1U), small samples (max 1.5U; under 5 recent maps 0), a month or more without an official map (max 1U) and thin betting markets (max 1.5U) cap the stake; a player not on the listed team and an AWPer on a headshot line get 0 units. An unconfirmed roster is flagged on the card but does not change the stake. Several bets that need the same team to do well (or badly) in one match share a 3U cap.

Tested before it counts. A factor moves the projection only after it beats the current model on matches it never saw, in date order. Maps, head-to-head, Round Swing, ratings, opening and trade kills, win chance and FACEIT are on every card as context; they move the grade only if they pass that test.

What the grades mean (chance the pick wins): A+ 80%+ · A 74%+ · A− 68%+ · B+ 63%+ · B 57%+ · B− 55%+ · Lean = 0 units: under 55%, or a stake rule above held it at 0.
Units: A+ 3U · A 2U · A− 1.5U · B+ 1U · B 0.5U · B− 0.5U · Lean 0U. The chance is graded unrounded and shown to 0.1%.

Grades are probabilities, not guarantees – a 60% pick still loses 4 times in 10. Not financial advice.
