How grades work

Built from official pro matches. Map 1, Maps 1–2 and Maps 1–3 lines use our performance model on every official map a player has played on HLTV: his kills per round, with recent months counting most (a map from about three months ago counts half), weighted toward his numbers in tonight's setting (LAN or online), against how many kills the opponent gives up. Headshot lines add his headshots per kill. A player with a short history is pulled toward the pro average until his own maps take over. Maps 1–2 kills lines use the complete model: the same kill rates plus opponent-adjusted kills, both teams' Elo ratings and how long their maps run, fitted together on past matches. FACEIT games never count.

Maps 1–3 lines count maps 1, 2 and 3 in full – never maps 4–5. In a best-of-5 all three are always played; in a best-of-3 that ends 2–0 there is no map 3 and Underdog voids the pick. When the performance model has no data for a player, the older model fills in (also on three full maps).

Projection → chance → grade. The projection is compared with the line (a 15.5 line needs 16 for an Over, 15 or less for an Under; on a whole line like 16, exactly 16 is a push) and turned into a chance from how much a player's totals normally swing from match to match. Kills chances are capped at 78% because big kill totals swing more than any model expects.

Stake rules change the units, never the chance. If his last 10 matches clearly disagree at this line, the stake drops to 0 (FORM FIGHT) or 0.5U (TAPE FIGHT). Stand-ins (max 1U), small samples (max 1.5U; under 5 recent maps 0), a month or more without an official map (max 1U) and the market-quality rule (a bookmaker price such as Thunderpick's, a thin exchange book or no betting market: max 1.5U) cap the stake; a player not on the listed team and an AWPer on a headshot line get 0 units. Several bets that need the same team to do well (or badly) in one match share a 3U cap.

Tested before it counts. A factor moves the projection only after it beats the current model on matches it never saw, in date order. Maps, head-to-head, Round Swing, ratings, opening and trade kills, win chance and FACEIT are on every card as context; they move the grade only if they pass that test.

What the grades mean (the model's estimated chance the pick wins): A+ 80%+ · A 74%+ · A− 68%+ · B+ 63%+ · B 57%+ · B− 55%+ · Lean = under 55%. A stake rule above can hold a graded pick at 0 units - it keeps its grade and says why (e.g. "B · 59.1% · 0U — same-match exposure cap"); 0-unit picks are never in "Bets only" or built slips.
Units: A+ 3U · A 2U · A− 1.5U · B+ 1U · B 0.5U · B− 0.5U · Lean 0U. The chance is graded unrounded and shown to 0.1%.

Every chance on this site is the model's estimate, not a measured hit rate – it has not yet been shown to match how often picks actually win, and a 60% pick still loses 4 times in 10 even when the estimate is right. The units are the grader's stake scale: a relative size set by the grade and the stake rules above, not a tested or optimal bankroll plan.
