How it works

  1. 01

    The data comes in

    Match and game data for every player, plus the live lines from Underdog, PrizePicks and the books, refreshed through the day.

  2. 02

    A chance at the exact line

    The model projects each player and turns it into the chance of the Over and the Under at the number you can play, not a rounded one.

  3. 03

    You choose

    Pick your side, build the slip and copy it to your platform. Every pick is logged before the game and settled after it.
