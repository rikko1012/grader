    01 · Why Grader

    Most prop tools stop at the grid. Grader shows its work.

    You still get the hit counts. You also get the reason for each pick, the exact line it was graded at, and the slip already built.

    Six apps, a stat site, a spreadsheet and a group chat.One board, one card, one slip.

    • Find the line

      Six apps open in six tabs

      PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, Dabble and the sportsbooks on one row per player, with a chip when two apps list different lines

    • Know why it's a pick

      A screenshot from a group chat

      Why Grader leans Over or Under, in plain English: a letter grade, a chance at that exact line and the reason for its confidence

    • See how often he clears it

      A stat site and your own spreadsheet

      L5, L10, L20, head-to-head and season, against this exact line, on the card

    • Check he's actually playing

      Roster posts and socials

      NBA and NFL games carry the ESPN injury report. CS2 lines count active players only, and stand-ins are flagged with their confidence capped

    • Catch a moved line

      Refresh and try to remember

      Line history from the opening line, with every move and when it happened

    • Build the slip

      Copy picks across by hand and hope nothing moved

      Slip Studio builds your slips, rechecks every line first and opens them in the app
