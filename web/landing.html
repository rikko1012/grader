Inside Scout

Everything behind a pick, on one screen

Board

Esports first. NBA and NFL too.

CS2 kills and headshots from HLTV pro match history, and League of Legends kills, assists and deaths, on the same board as NBA and NFL. Every line carries a letter grade.

  • HLTV map chances and the likeliest map veto on CS2 cards
  • Roles under every CS2 name: AWPer, IGL, Entry, Lurker
  • Stand-ins flagged on CS2 lines, with their stake capped
Board

The player card

The reason behind every grade

Open any player for the lean, the letter grade, the stake and the reason, with the numbers that back it up.

  • Hits at this exact line over L5, L10, L20, head-to-head and season
  • The stake reason spelled out: stand-in, small sample, long break
  • Ask the Genie: it answers from that line's own saved numbers

Every book

Every app's line side by side

PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper and Dabble for all four sports. For NBA and NFL, DraftKings, FanDuel, BetRivers, Bovada and Unibet prices and Kalshi and Polymarket markets sit beside the pick'em line.

  • A chip when two apps list different lines
  • Line history: every move since the line opened
  • The +EV board: legs the books price above what your entry needs to break even

Slips

Slips built for you

Tell Slip Studio how many. Auto-build makes 1 to 12 PrizePicks or Underdog slips of 2 to 6 picks each from the lines open right now, and rechecks every line before you see them.

  • Each slip shows the chance every pick hits and the payout
  • Open the slip in the app, or edit it in My slip first
  • Slips save to your account, with a receipt when they settle
