    How reviews work
    • Only paying Prop Grader members, signed in with Whop, can write a review. Each one shows a Verified member badge.
    • One review per member. You can edit or delete yours at any time; an edited review shows the date it was edited.
    • Every rating is posted, from 1 to 5 stars. A review is removed only for spam, slurs or abuse, or personal info, whatever its rating.
    • No one is paid or rewarded for writing a review.
    • The average and the star breakdown count every posted review.
