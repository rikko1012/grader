Questions

Before you join.

Is this a picks group?

No. Scout is a board you read yourself. Every graded prop shows Scout's chance at the exact line, a letter grade and the reasons in plain English. You decide what to play.

How do I know the grades are honest?

Every graded call is saved before the game starts and scored after it on the public record, losses included. Each sport states its rule, and anyone can read it without an account. {{UNREC}} are graded on the board but are not on the public record yet.

Will I win every day?

No. A grade is a chance, not a certainty, and the record shows the losing days next to the good ones. Parlays multiply the risk, which is why Slip Studio prints the chance that every leg hits.

Does it cover my app?

Today's boards carry lines from {{BOOK_LIST}}, and Scout grades the line each app actually lists.

Is esports an afterthought?

No. CS2 came first: HLTV map chances and veto history, roles, stand-ins and headshots all feed the CS2 card, and LoL has its own model. {{SPORT_LIST}} share one board.

What happens after I pay?

Checkout runs on Whop. Then sign in with Whop on this page and the board, player pages, Slip Studio and your saved picks open in the browser, on phone or desktop.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from your Whop account.
