03 · The tools

The sharp side of the board.

A grade belongs to a line, not a player. Everything below exists so you play the right one.

Line gaps

When apps disagree, see it.

A chip marks every prop where two apps list different lines. Scout grades each line on its own, so a half point shows up as a different chance.

{{GAP_MINI}}

Line moves

Know the opener.

When an app bumps a line, the row keeps the opening number beside the new one, and line history shows every move with its time.

{{MOVE_MINI}}

Break-even

Chance against price.

Each entry type pays differently. Scout prints the chance each leg needs for your entry beside its own chance, and Slip Studio prints the chance every leg hits.

{{BE_MINI}}

Best prices

Market fair, at the app's line.

Where two independent books price the exact same line, Scout shows their fair chance beside its own and the break-even for your entry. One feed never counts twice.

Price check

Both sides, two books.

The price check lists a pair only when two different books price the identical line, with each quote's age. Quotes go stale fast and the sheet says so.

05 · Questions

Before you join.

Is this a picks group?

No. Scout is a board you read yourself. Every graded prop shows Scout's chance at the exact line, a letter grade and the reasons in plain English. You decide what to play.

How do I know the grades are honest?

Every graded call is saved before the game starts and scored after it on the public record, losses included. The record states its rule and its start date, and anyone can read it without an account.

Will I win every day?

No. A grade is a chance, not a certainty, and the record shows the losing days next to the good ones. Parlays multiply the risk, which is why Slip Studio prints the chance that every leg hits.

Does it cover my app?

Scout reads PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, Dabble, DraftKings, FanDuel, BetRivers, Unibet, Kalshi and Polymarket, and grades the line each app actually lists.

Is esports an afterthought?

No. CS2 came first: HLTV map chances and veto history, roles, stand-ins and headshots all feed the CS2 card, and League has its own model. {{SPORT_LIST}} share one board.

What happens after I pay?

Checkout runs on Whop. Then sign in with Whop on this page and the board, player cards, Slip Studio and your saved picks open in the browser, on phone or desktop.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from your Whop account.
