Questions

Before you start.

Is this a picks group?

No. Prop Grader is a board you read yourself. Every graded prop shows the model estimate at the exact line, a letter grade and the reasons in plain English. You decide what to play.

What happens after my free day?

Your membership renews at {{PRICE_M}}/month unless you cancel from your Whop account before the free day ends.

How do I know the grades are honest?

Every graded call is saved before the game starts and scored after it on the public record, losses included. Each sport states its rule, and anyone can read it without an account. {{UNREC_SENT}}

Will I win every day?

No. A grade is a chance, not a certainty, and the record shows the losing days next to the good ones. Parlays multiply the risk, which is why Slip Studio prints the estimate that every leg hits.

Do I need a big bankroll?

No. Grades are chances, not promises, so play only what you can afford to lose and stay within your limits. For adults 21 and over. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Which apps does it cover?

The boards carry lines from {{BOOK_LIST}}, and Prop Grader grades the line each app actually lists.

Is esports an afterthought?

No. CS2 came first: HLTV map chances and veto history, roles, stand-ins and headshots all feed the CS2 card, and LoL has its own model. {{SPORT_LIST}} share one board.

What happens after I sign up?

Checkout runs on Whop. Then sign in with Whop on this page and the board, player pages, Slip Studio and your saved picks open in the browser, on phone or desktop.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from your Whop account.
