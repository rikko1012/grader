LoL member workspace and grading-evidence repair
Base: origin/main 4e77d48. Branch: codex/lol-ui-accuracy-20261022.

Member UI
- Responsive research cards with an optional horizontally scrollable table.
- Navigation separated from book, prop, scope, model-side and search filters.
- Compact phone controls; secondary filters expand on request.
- Counts reflect the filtered board. Ready picks must be offered, upcoming and recently checked.
- First 48 matching lines render initially; Show more retains the full filtered set.
- Compare books filters the actual offers, not just the enclosing player row.
- Whole-number push probability travels through the comparison serializer and is displayed.
- Player research has a visible top Close button, a bottom return button, Escape and a keyboard focus loop.

Correctness
- CS2 picklog.report no longer crashes on settled rows missing probability/outcome.
  Unknown outcomes are not counted as losses. Calibration compares identical cohorts
  with valid probabilities and outcomes; exclusions are counted on the admin page.
  Its bands now separate Lean and B- at the actual 55% threshold. The threaded
  read test now fails when the report raises, instead of passing with a thread warning.
- A successful cached-board HTTP response is not treated as a new quote check.
- Manual copy/add and server slip rechecks reject quote checks older than 15 minutes,
  missing check times, times more than one minute in the future, and missing starts.
  The 15-minute rule matches the pre-existing stale-feed warning. Existing availability,
  stake, same-match and player-overlap restrictions remain in force.
- LoL learning reports split model version, fit and offered/unoffered selected side.
- An explicit offered-picks-printed-60%+ cohort is recorded, including per-model cohorts.
- The evidence counter uses distinct series/player/stat/scope units, not alternate lines
  or repeated book offers. Existing aggregate metrics remain for compatibility.
- Probabilities scored on non-push results are explicitly described as conditional on no push.

No projection coefficients, grade thresholds or stakes were changed. These repairs improve
usability, input checks and measurement; they do not establish a predictive accuracy gain.
No 60% guarantee is made. Keep version-separated forward scoring; any future model promotion
must use its registered comparison and untouched forecasts rather than an attractive recent win rate.
