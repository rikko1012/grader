## Rules for every video

1. Every pick shown on camera comes from today's live section (verified saved forecast, line offered right now). Never type a pick in by hand.
2. Say the chance as the grader's model estimate ("62.4% model estimate"), never as a hit rate, a lock or a guarantee.
3. No profit, ROI or record claims about the grader. If you show results, show the Results tab exactly as it is, losses included.
4. Never pick a stat window because it sounds strong ("hit in 9 of his last 10"). The only allowed context is what the live script gives you.
5. Lean and 0-unit lines are not picks. Show them only as examples of the grader saying "pass".
6. Every video and caption ends with: "Research tool, not betting advice."
7. Lines move. Film the line check (the platform showing the same number) and post the same day; if the line moved, re-pull the live section.
8. Never target or feature anyone under 21; no school settings, no cartoon styles aimed at kids.

## Know the product (read this first)

### What 1Time is

- A research tool for CS2 and League of Legends player props on Underdog and PrizePicks, sold as a Whop membership (whop.com/1timeesports).
- It grades every posted line: our projected number, the chance of each side, a letter grade and a suggested stake.
- It is research, not betting advice and not a tipster service. It never places bets.

### Where the numbers come from (CS2)

- Match history from HLTV (every official map), refreshed every 2 hours. BO3.gg fills in only when HLTV has no history for a player.
- Lines are read straight from the platforms: Underdog every 5 minutes, PrizePicks every 2 minutes.
- Each line is graded automatically within minutes of appearing; a moved line is regraded before it is shown again.

### How a CS2 grade is made

- Projection: the player's kills (or headshots) per round, weighted toward recent maps, adjusted for the opponent, the match environment (LAN or online) and expected rounds - for the exact maps in the prop (Map 1, Maps 1-2 or Maps 1-3).
- Chance: the probability of Over and Under at that exact line from the projection's spread. Whole-number lines can push; the push chance is shown separately.
- Grade from the chance of the chosen side: A+ 80%+, A 74%+, A- 68%+, B+ 63%+, B 57%+, B- 55%+. Under 55% = Lean (not a pick).
- Kills chances are capped at 78% (very high printed kills chances did not hold up), so kills can't show A+.
- Stake: A+ 3U, A 2U, A- 1.5U, B+ 1U, B and B- 0.5U. Caps: stand-in max 1U; fewer than 5 recent maps = 0U; back from a long break max 1U; a weak betting market for the match can cap the stake too.
- Every grade carries the model version that made it. Grades are saved before the match so results can be checked honestly.

### What members see and use

- Board: every graded line, filterable by platform, stat, maps, Overs/Unders; sorted by grade, chance or start time.
- Chips on a line: 'bumped from X' (the platform moved the line since it opened) and '≠ other platform X' (the other app lists a different line for the same prop) - which side the gap helps is shown on hover.
- Matches: every line of one match together, with a 'Research match' view.
- Compare: the same prop on both platforms side by side.
- Player card: Overview, History, Match and Markets tabs; the distribution chart (how likely each total is); nearby lines.
- Slips: add picks, see the chance all legs hit (pushes counted correctly), and a recheck that refuses a line that moved, was pulled or changed platform. Slip studio builds slips from graded picks with overlap rules; Slip history shows how they did.
- Results: how the board's picks did - the average printed chance next to how often they actually won (a push counts as won).
- Moneylines: series-winner prices from exchanges (market prices, not our prediction).
- Tools: Single prop (grade any line you type) and Slate (paste a whole slate).
- League of Legends: kills, assists and deaths lines with a projection, chance and grade per game scope; the card lists every factor (role, form, team strength, lane opponent, game pace, patch, rest...) and the team's win chance (model and market). LoL stakes are 0U until enough real lines have settled.

### What the grader is NOT

- Not a guarantee: a 65% grade loses about 1 time in 3. Losing days happen.
- Not a record or profit service: never quote a win rate, ROI or $ figure for the grader. The Results tab is the only results source.
- Not a lock picker: never say lock, sure thing, free money, can't lose.
- Not aware of everything: late stand-ins, the draft (LoL) and live game events are not known before the match.

### Words to use / avoid

- Use: 'graded', 'model estimate', 'the grader projects', 'research', 'the line', 'Lean = pass'.
- Avoid: 'lock', 'guaranteed', 'easy money', 'hit rate' (for a single pick), 'we're up X units', anything aimed at under-21s.
- Always end: 'Research tool, not betting advice.'
